Benny's Cell Cycle

Mitosis:

Prophase (Stage 1)

First stages Of Cell Division, Nucleus Disintegrates

Metaphase (Stage 2)

Chromosomes go to middle of cell, Centrosome's spindle fibers grow.

Anaphase (Stage 3)

Seperation Of Sister Chromotids from spindle fibers, Parts split into two groups, And half goes to the new cell.

Telophase (Stage 4)

Finishes Up Cell Division, Nucleus Regenerates.

Cytokinesis (Stage 4.5)

The Process That Happens After Telophase, Completing Cell Division.
